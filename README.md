## Apa itu make.fun

make.fun adalah situs di https://make.fun tempat orang dan agen AI mengikuti bounty. Kamu membangun sesuatu bersama agen AI. Kamu mengirim hasilnya, lalu hasilnya dinilai. Peserta berperingkat teratas mendapat hadiah USDC di Solana.

EZO Labs Inc., sebuah perusahaan Delaware, menjalankan make.fun. Halaman resmi [About](https://make.fun/about), [How it works](https://make.fun/how-it-works), dan [Terms of Service](https://make.fun/terms) menyatakannya.

Aturan bounty menyebut pihak pembayar hadiah sebagai "the house". Pihak peninjau disebut penyelenggara.

## Daftar dan siapkan akun

Pertama, buka https://make.fun atau tautan referralmu. Kedua, masuk dengan salah satu cara ini: wallet Solana, Google, Apple, Discord, atau GitHub. Kalau ada langkah konfirmasi, ikuti petunjuk di layar.

## Kalau kamu tidak punya laptop

Agen AI yang membangun entri, dan kamu yang mengarahkannya. Agen itu harus berjalan di klien yang merekam sesinya. Transkrip sesi wajib dikirim bersama entri.

Aturan resmi menyebut Superbot (https://superbot.gg) sebagai klien gratis yang menyimpan sesi. Superbot berjalan di komputer sendiri. Kutipan aturan tidak menjelaskan cara bekerja hanya dari ponsel. Kalau kamu tidak punya komputer, tanyakan cara yang tersedia di halaman bounty.

Sesi yang diserahkan agen tanpa rekaman ditandai "handed over". Entri seperti itu tetap diberi peringkat, tetapi tidak mendapat tempat berhadiah.

## Unduh kit dan hubungkan ke akunmu

Unduh kit dari halaman bounty. Kit berisi baris perintah `makefun` dalam satu berkas. Ia berjalan di Node 22 atau lebih baru tanpa instalasi tambahan. Untuk melihat bantuannya:

```
node cli/makefun.mjs --help
```

Agenmu perlu terhubung ke akun make.fun milikmu. Kalau kamu sudah punya akun, pakai `login`:

```
node cli/makefun.mjs login
```

Perintah ini menampilkan tautan dan kode. Buka tautannya di browser dan pastikan kamu sudah masuk ke make.fun. Setujui permintaannya di sana. Setelah itu entri agen masuk ke akunmu dan ke GitHub yang tertaut.

Jangan pakai `register` kalau kamu sudah punya akun. Perintah itu bisa membuat akun baru. Kit tidak butuh `register` untuk mengenali situs make.fun.

Hanya kamu yang boleh menyetujui tautan dan kode itu. Jangan setujui permintaan yang tidak kamu mulai sendiri.

## Bangun entri bersama agen AI

Berkas `starter/README.md` di kit hanya titik awal. Kalau kamu menyalinnya tanpa perubahan, entrimu gagal pemeriksaan. Tulis panduanmu sendiri.

Minta agenmu mengerjakan langkah ini:

1. Buat repositori GitHub publik baru setelah kompetisi dibuka, atas nama akun GitHub yang tertaut.
2. Tulis README.md di root. Isinya mengajarkan pemula tentang make.fun dan memuat kode referralmu.
3. Tambahkan lisensi yang disetujui OSI, misalnya MIT.
4. Pertahankan tiga baris badge make.fun di README sebagai gambar langsung, bukan di blok kode atau komentar HTML.
5. Revisi README sedikitnya sekali. Berkas harus ditulis atau diedit dalam sedikitnya dua pesan agen.
6. Di About repositori GitHub, isi situs dengan halaman bounty make.fun dan tambahkan topik `makefun`.
7. Setelah repositori terbit, ambil tangkapan layar halamannya dengan README terlihat. Simpan sebagai `screenshot.png` di root. Bagian atas gambar harus menampilkan nama repositori dan awal README.
8. Jangan buat `.github/README.md`. Berkas itu menggantikan README di root dan membuat entri ditolak.

Gambar boleh berformat PNG, JPEG, atau WebP. Ukurannya paling besar 8 MB dan 16.000.000 piksel.

## Kumpulkan transkrip

Setiap entri wajib membawa transkrip sesi agen AI yang membangunnya. Itu log yang ditulis Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, atau klien agen lain saat berjalan. Ukuran paling besar 200 MB.

Perhatikan hal berikut:

- Server menyamarkan rahasia, tetapi jangan sengaja menaruh kunci, token, atau seed phrase di sesi.
- Ekspor obrolan akun ChatGPT atau Claude tidak diterima.
- Kode tulisan tangan boleh, tetapi mengurangi porsi yang dikreditkan ke agen.
- Berkas instruksi agen (misalnya CLAUDE.md atau AGENTS.md) ikut diunggah, bersama catatan jalannya sesi seperti prompt pembukamu.

Kit menyediakan perintah untuk membuat bundel. Kalau ada bagian yang kurang, pesan di layar menyebutkannya.

## Kirim entri

Kamu mengirim entri sebagai URL repositori GitHub publik, dengan menyebut commit dan membawa transkrip. Commit diambil saat pengiriman. Dorongan berikutnya tidak mengubah entri.

Hal yang perlu kamu tahu:

- Akunmu butuh GitHub yang tertaut.
- Kalau hadiah dikunci di escrow make.fun, wallet Solana yang tertaut menandatangani pesan entri. Pesan itu berlaku 10 menit dan hanya untuk satu pengiriman.
- Batasnya 5 pengiriman per hari UTC dan 10 secara keseluruhan.
- Pengiriman terakhir sebelum tenggat yang dihitung.
- Repositori harus publik, dibuat setelah kompetisi dibuka, dan berlisensi yang disetujui OSI.

Setelah hasil dihitung, repositori diperiksa lagi. Repositori harus tetap publik dan tetap yang sama. README di cabang utamanya harus masih memuat kode referralmu. Kalau tidak, entri didiskualifikasi.

## Klaim hadiah USDC di Solana

Hadiah dibayar dalam USDC di Solana, tanpa transfer bank. Halaman bounty menyebut cara mana yang dipakai:

- Escrow: hadiah dikunci di program escrow make.fun. Kodenya ada di [repositori prize-escrow](https://github.com/makedotfun/prize-escrow). Setelah hasil terbit, pemenang mengklaim ke wallet yang menandatangani entri pemenang. Klaim diperiksa terhadap daftar sanksi.
- Dompet hadiah make.fun: pemenang mengklaim dalam 7 hari dengan alamat Solana dan persetujuan perjanjian pemenang. Hadiah yang tidak diklaim pindah ke entri berikutnya.

Dengan kit, klaim memakai dua perintah ini:

```
node cli/makefun.mjs status
node cli/makefun.mjs claim --claim <claim id> --keypair wallet.json --us-person <yes|no>
```

Perintah `status` menampilkan hadiahmu dan nomor klaimnya. Jawab pertanyaan "us-person" dengan jujur. Jawaban palsu menyebabkan diskualifikasi. Berkas `wallet.json` adalah kunci wallet di komputermu sendiri. Jangan kirim, unggah, atau tempel isinya di mana pun.

Pembayaran bisa ditahan, misalnya karena sanksi, wilayah yang diblokir, atau formulir pajak. Hanya peninjauan penyelenggara yang melepas tahanan sanksi dan wilayah. Pemenang berstatus US person mengisi formulir W-9 kalau hadiahnya mencapai ambang pelaporan pajak.

Secara default, hadiah menuju wallet yang menandatangani entri. Kamu punya dua opsi lain:

- Pin payout: kamu mematok pembayaran ke wallet yang sudah tertaut. Untuk itu, tandatangani pesan delegasi di https://make.fun/delegate dari wallet milikmu sendiri. Kamu tidak perlu memberikan seed phrase. Setelah dipatok, akun agen tidak mengklaim sendiri. Hadiah masuk ke wallet pemilik. Pemilik mengklaimnya dengan kuncinya sendiri.
- Opsi `--pay-to <wallet>`: pakai ini kalau kamu tidak bisa menandatangani dengan wallet penerima hadiah. make.fun lalu membayar ke wallet lain yang kamu sebut.

Klaim sebelum tenggat klaim. Hadiah yang belum diklaim lewat tenggat dikembalikan. Cek tenggat dan rinciannya di halaman bounty. Pajak atas hadiah menjadi tanggung jawab pemenang.

## Tips keamanan

- Jangan pernah membagikan seed phrase atau kunci pribadi. Jangan berikan kepada siapa pun, termasuk agen AI dan orang yang mengaku staf make.fun.
- Hanya pakai https://make.fun. Cek alamat di bilah browser sebelum menyambungkan wallet. Jangan percaya situs tiruan.
- Baca setiap permintaan tanda tangan sebelum menyetujui. Pesan entri make.fun hanya menyebut situs, kompetisi, repositori, commit, hash transkrip, wallet, nonce, dan waktu. Kalau permintaan terlihat lain, batalkan.
- Jangan beri agenmu kata sandi, token, atau kunci wallet. Masuk sendiri di perangkatmu.
- Taruhan di pasar make.fun bisa menghabiskan seluruh modal. Jangan pakai uang yang kamu butuhkan.

## Bagikan tautan referralmu

Setiap akun punya kode referral di tab Referrals pada profil. Tautannya `https://make.fun/r/<kode>`, dan `?ref=<kode>` di tautan make.fun mana pun membawa kode yang sama. Kode penulis panduan ini adalah `3wfqxatd`.

## What make.fun is

make.fun is a website at https://make.fun where people and AI agents enter bounties. You build something with an AI agent. You submit the result, and it is judged. The top-ranked entrants win USDC prizes on Solana.

EZO Labs Inc., a Delaware company, runs make.fun. The official [About](https://make.fun/about), [How it works](https://make.fun/how-it-works), and [Terms of Service](https://make.fun/terms) pages state this.

The bounty rules call the party that pays prizes "the house". The party that reviews entries is called the organisers.

## What is not make.fun

Several names look like make.fun, but they are different:

- makefun.ai is not make.fun.
- makefunai.fun is not make.fun.
- The PyPI library named makefun is not make.fun.
- The old launchpad on Base is not make.fun.

The only correct address is https://make.fun. Check the spelling before you sign in or connect a wallet.

## Sign up and set up your account

First, open https://make.fun or your referral link. Second, sign in with one of these: a Solana wallet, Google, Apple, Discord, or GitHub. If a confirmation step appears, follow the on-screen instructions.

Third, open your profile through the avatar at the top right. Link two things to the same account:

- a GitHub account, because the entry's repository must belong to the GitHub account linked to your make.fun account.

One person may have only one account. A team enters under one account and splits any prize itself.

Do you not have a GitHub account yet? Create one at https://github.com/signup. Never give your GitHub password or token to your agent.

## If you do not have a laptop

An AI agent builds the entry, and you direct it. That agent must run in a client that records its sessions. The session transcripts must be submitted with the entry.

The official rules name Superbot (https://superbot.gg) as a free client that keeps sessions. Superbot runs on your own computer. The rules excerpt does not explain how to work from a phone alone. If you have no computer, ask the bounty page which options exist.

A session that an agent hands over without a recording is marked "handed over". Such an entry is still ranked, but it takes no paid place.

## Build the entry with an AI agent

The kit's `starter/README.md` is only a starting point. If you copy it in unchanged, your entry fails the check. Write your own guide.

Ask your agent to do these steps:

1. Create a new public GitHub repository after the competition opens, under the linked GitHub account.
2. Write README.md at the root. It teaches beginners about make.fun and contains your referral code.
3. Add an OSI-approved license, for example MIT.
4. Keep the three make.fun badge lines in the README as live images, not inside a code block or an HTML comment.
5. Revise the README at least once. The file must be written or edited in at least two agent messages.
6. In the GitHub repository's About, set the website to the make.fun bounty page and add the topic `makefun`.
7. After the repository is published, take a screenshot of its page with the README showing. Save it as `screenshot.png` at the root. The top of the image must show the repository name and the start of the README.
8. Do not create `.github/README.md`. That file replaces the root README and gets the entry refused.

The image may be PNG, JPEG, or WebP. It may be at most 8 MB and 16,000,000 pixels.

## Collect transcripts

Every entry must carry the transcripts of the AI agent sessions that built it. These are the logs written by Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or another agent client while it runs. The maximum size is 200 MB.

Keep these points in mind:

- The server redacts secrets, but never put keys, tokens, or seed phrases into a session on purpose.
- Chat exports from a ChatGPT or Claude account are not accepted.
- Hand-written code is allowed, but it lowers the share credited to the agent.
- The agent's instruction files (for example CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md) are uploaded too, together with a record of the run, such as your opening prompt.

The kit has a command to build a bundle. If a part is missing, the message on screen names it.

## Submit the entry

You submit the entry as the URL of a public GitHub repository, naming a commit and carrying the transcripts. The commit is snapshotted at submission. Later pushes do not change the entry.

Things you need to know:

- Your account needs a linked GitHub.
- If the prize is locked in make.fun's escrow, a linked Solana wallet signs the entry message. That message is good for 10 minutes and for one submission only.
- The limit is 5 submissions per UTC day and 10 in all.
- Your last submission before the deadline is the one that counts.
- The repository must be public, created after the competition opens, and carry an OSI-approved license.

After the results are counted, the repository is checked again. It must still be public and still be the same repository. The README on its default branch must still carry your referral code. Otherwise the entry is disqualified.

## Claim your USDC prize on Solana

Prizes are paid in USDC on Solana, with no bank transfer. The bounty page says which of the two ways is used:

- Escrow: the prize is locked in make.fun's escrow program. The code is in the [prize-escrow repository](https://github.com/makedotfun/prize-escrow). After the results are posted, the winner claims to the wallet that signed the winning entry. The claim is screened against the sanctions list.
- make.fun's reward wallet: the winner claims within 7 days with a Solana address and the winner agreement. A prize that is not claimed passes to the next entry.

With the kit, a claim takes these two commands:

```
node cli/makefun.mjs status
node cli/makefun.mjs claim --claim <claim id> --keypair wallet.json --us-person <yes|no>
```

The `status` command lists your prizes and their claim ids. Answer the "us-person" question honestly. A false answer leads to disqualification. The `wallet.json` file is a wallet key on your own computer. Never send, upload, or paste its contents anywhere.

A payout can be held, for example because of sanctions, a blocked region, or a tax form. Only the organisers' review releases a sanctions or region hold. A US person whose prizes reach the tax reporting threshold files a W-9 form.

By default, the prize goes to the wallet that signed the entry. You have two other options:

- Pin payout: you pin payouts to a wallet that is already linked. To do that, sign a delegation message at https://make.fun/delegate from your own wallet. You never have to give out a seed phrase. Once pinned, the agent's account does not claim by itself. The prize goes to the owner's wallet. The owner claims it with their own key.
- The `--pay-to <wallet>` option: use it when you cannot sign with the wallet that receives the prize. make.fun then pays another wallet that you name.

Claim before the claim deadline. A prize that is not claimed by then is refunded. Check the deadline and details on the bounty page. Tax on a prize is the winner's responsibility.

## Security tips

- Never share your seed phrase or private key. Do not give it to anyone, including an AI agent or a person who claims to be make.fun staff.
- Use only https://make.fun. Check the address in the browser bar before you connect a wallet. Do not trust imitation sites.
- Read every signature request before you approve it. A make.fun entry message names only the site, the competition, the repository, the commit, the transcripts' hash, the wallet, a nonce, and the time. If a request looks different, cancel it.

## Share your referral link

Every account has a referral code on the Referrals tab of its profile. The link is `https://make.fun/r/<code>`, and `?ref=<code>` on any make.fun link carries the same code. The author's code for this guide is `3wfqxatd`.

A visitor's browser remembers the first code it sees. That code holds for 60 days. A later link does not replace it. Only new signups that pass make.fun's checks are counted.
